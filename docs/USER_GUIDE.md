**For:** Marketing, Sales, HR — anyone who needs branded social posts
**Access:** http://[LAN-IP]:3000
**Start the tool:** Double-click `start_studio.bat` on Umang's laptop

---

## Making Your First Post (90 seconds)

1. **Choose Post Theme** — Dark (default, industrial feel) or Light (clean, minimal)
2. **Choose Logo** — NowPurchase, MetalCloud, or Combined (both logos)
3. **Fill in your brief:**
   - **Post Type** — what kind of post is this?
   - **Headline** *(required)* — be specific, use numbers. "Reduce scrap by 23%" beats "Improve quality"
   - **Stat** — the big number if your post leads with data
   - **Body Copy** — 1–2 supporting lines max
4. Click **Generate Post**

The AI generates your post in ~3 minutes. You'll see live progress.

---

## Reviewing Your Post

When the post appears:
- **Compliance score** — 80+ means it passes brand standards automatically
- Use the **chat** to request changes: "Make the headline bigger", "Use a different background", "Switch to light mode"
- Common revision chips are shown — click to apply instantly
- **Approve & Download** when you're happy — saves the PNG directly

## Getting Good Results

**Do:**
- Give specific numbers: "18% cost reduction" not "significant savings"
- Match headline length to post type — stat-forward posts need short headlines
- Use MetalCloud logo for product posts

**Don't:**
- Write more than 2 lines of body copy (it won't fit)
- Use vague headlines ("Transforming foundries") — the AI will score them low
- Use emojis — the brand voice is authoritative, not casual
